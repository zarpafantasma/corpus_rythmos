## Document Summaries

### 016 - Aetherion, The Jumper

**Core mechanism:** Spatial gradients ∇α distort the local vacuum dispersion relation, lifting a fraction of zero-point energy into an accessible metastable band.

**Chapter I — Vacuum Energy Extraction:**
- Introduces the Aetherion field φ that couples to ∇α
- Power density scales as P ∝ (∇α)² ε₀
- Red Team reclassification: static extraction models are "Topological Capacitors" — they store vacuum stress rather than generating continuous DC power
- Manufacturable chamber: metamaterial layers imposing α-gradient from ≈2 (diffusive) to ≈3 (holographic)

**Chapter II — Propulsion:**
- Asymmetric α-profiles generate unidirectional thrust: F/A ∝ |∇α| ε_ZPE
- No propellant required — momentum transfer from structured vacuum
- Pulsed gradient sequences for discrete spatial "hops"

**Chapter III — Branch Jumps:**
- If α is quantized, what governs transitions between bands?
- Introduces branch-index field β parameterizing which α-well a region occupies
- 3D Nucleation Thresholds: "multiversal surface tension" prohibits micro-scale transitions
- **Macroscopic Mandate:** jump stability only achieved in cores exceeding ~1 meter radius

### 017 - RTM Unified Field Framework

**Key move:** α becomes a dynamical scalar field, not a static parameter.

**Field theory construction:**
- Effective action where α couples to spacetime curvature and matter fields
- Multi-well potential V(α) anchors α at quantized bands (≈1, 2, 2.5, 3.5)
- β-functions computed at one-loop level confirm stability of fixed points
- Framework reduces to established physics in appropriate limits: Klein-Gordon, Einstein field equations, original RTM power law

**α-φ coupling:**
- The term g_αφ(∇α)²φ² lowers the barrier in V(α) when φ is large
- This provides the mechanism for engineered metamaterials to induce controlled α-transitions
- Aetherion program embedded as primary experimental validation target

**Falsifiable predictions:**
- Casimir-analog forces between α-discontinuities
- Equivalence-principle violations at precision level
- Holographic probes of time-flow anomalies
- Multi-modal signatures from Aetherion chamber prototypes

### 018 - The Aetherion Framework and Implications

**Status:** Pre-emptive operational manual — "a document waiting for its reality to catch up."

**Multiverse architecture:**
- Not parallel independent realities, but a **sequential causal cascade** (dominoes)
- Unidirectionality of ontological flow — movement only "forward" in the sequence
- Each echo is sovereign but causally determined by the preceding branch

**The Russian Doll Postulate:**
- Each subsequent echo manifests at smaller physical scale: L_{n+1} = k·L_n (k < 1)
- Time runs faster in subsequent branches (consequence of T ∝ L^α)

**The Godzilla Scaling Paradox:**
- First jump: travelers arrive as "Titans" — manageable discrepancy, mythological scale
- Second jump: travelers are true giants — "surgery with construction cranes"
- Third jump: travelers are Godzilla-sized — landing is an extinction-level event

**Operational protocols outlined:**
- N-1 Architect role (civilizational guidance)
- BioDrone interface requirements
- Isotopic Anchors for navigation
- Asset Sterilization Protocols
