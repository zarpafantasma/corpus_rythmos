## What is Aetherion?

In classical RTM, transport between scales follows continuous power-law scaling (τ ∝ L^α). **Aetherion theory** asks: what happens at the boundaries? What mediates the *transition* between transport classes?

The Aetherion is proposed as:
- A **topological defect** in scale-space where α becomes undefined or multivalued
- A **transport mediator** enabling discontinuous "jumps" across scale hierarchies
- A potential **unification mechanism** linking quantum and classical regimes through scale-geometry

Think of it as the RTM equivalent of asking: "If spacetime has curvature (gravity), does scale-space have its own geometry — and what are its singularities?"
