## 9. Three Rules of Sanity

### 9.1 Rule One

```
RULE ONE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    DO NOT BREAK THINGS JUST TO PROVE A POINT.
    ───────────────────────────────────────────
    
    
    IF SOMETHING OPENS, IT IS TENDED TO.
    ────────────────────────────────────
    
    Not smashed.
    Not abandoned.
    Not exploited.
    
    TENDED TO.
    
    
    WHY:
    ────
    Even if this is a simulation,
    consequences are real within it.
    Pain is real. Damage is real. Harm is real.
    
    Breaking things to prove a point
    proves nothing except the breaker's immaturity.
    
    
    THE PRACTICE:
    ─────────────
    When you discover something vulnerable:
    Care for it.
    When you find a bug:
    Report it, don't exploit it.
    When a door opens:
    Enter with respect.

```

### 9.2 Rule Two

```
RULE TWO

    ─────────────────────────────
    
    
    THE PRACTICE:
    ─────────────
    
    MEASURE as if everything were a machine.
    
    CREATE as if the world learned.
    
    LISTEN as if everything meant something.
    
    
    ALL THREE. ALWAYS.
    
    
    WHY:
    ────
    Each layer captures something real.
    Missing any layer leaves you blind to that aspect.
    
    The machine-only view misses meaning.
    The maturation-only view misses constraint.
    The information-only view misses embodiment.
    
    
    THE DISCIPLINE:
    ───────────────
    In every situation, ask:
    • What are the constraints? (machine)
    • What is developing? (maturation)
    • What does it mean? (information)
    
    Never settle for one answer.
    Use all three microphones.

```
RULE THREE
