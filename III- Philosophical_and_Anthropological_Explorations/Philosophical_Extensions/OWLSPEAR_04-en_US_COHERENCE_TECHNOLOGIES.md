    WE DO:
    ──────

## 9. The Hydraulic Confusion

### 9.1 The Most Common Confusion

Perhaps the most common confusion is not theological, but hydraulic:

```
THE HYDRAULIC CONFUSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    THE CONFUSION:
    ──────────────
    We ask the dam for the sea.
    We ask the river for the dam.
    We ask the sea for the river.
    
    
    EXAMPLES:
    ─────────
    
    DEMANDING THE SEA FROM A DAM:
    Asking an administrative structure
    to grant us the freshness of a torrent.
    
    "Why is this institution not giving me direct experience?"
    
    
    DEMANDING THE DAM FROM A RIVER:
    Asking a local tradition
    to carry the memory of centuries on its back.
    
    "Why doesn't this small practice have universal authority?"
    
    
    DEMANDING THE RIVER FROM THE SEA:
    Asking a path of silence
    to resolve the logistics of a people.
    
    "Why doesn't this mystical teaching organize society?"
    
    
    EACH OPERATION HAS ITS PURPOSE:
    ────────────────────────────────
    Confusing them produces suffering.
    Remembering their function simplifies the journey.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### 9.2 What Each Is For

```
WHAT EACH IS FOR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    THE DAM:
    ────────
    Prevents famines and spiritual droughts.
    
    Stores for the dry season.
    Distributes to the many.
    Provides stability.
    
    
    THE RIVER:
    ──────────
    Brings nutrition to the periphery.
    Keeps the landscape alive.
    
    Oxygenates.
    Moves.
    Brings freshness.
    
    
    THE SEA:
    ────────
    Returns form to its source.
    And, with it, the learning of having been a drop.
    
    Dissolves edges.
    Reveals unity.
    Completes the cycle.
    
    
    USE EACH FOR ITS PURPOSE:
    ─────────────────────────
    Don't ask the dam to be a sea.
    Don't ask the river to be a dam.
    Don't ask the sea to be a river.
    
    Know what you need.
    Go to the right source.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

    ──────────────
    "Here are the religions. Pick one."
    
    
    BUT GEOGRAPHY:
    ──────────────

    Rivers.
    Dams.
    Estuaries.

    ─────────────────────────────────
