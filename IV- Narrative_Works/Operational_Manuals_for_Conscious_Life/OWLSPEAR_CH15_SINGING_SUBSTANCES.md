       Does it increase sustainable coherence or negotiate immediate peak?
       Not moral. Mechanical.
    
    6. THE LIMITS:
       Opening too much wounds. Confusing vision with mandate builds sects.
       Chasing peaks wears out the tool.
    
    7. ENDOGENOUS KEYS:
       Breathing, fasting, vigils, silence.
       The goal is graduation: recognizing the tone without help.
    
    8. TUNING FORKS, NOT THRONES:
