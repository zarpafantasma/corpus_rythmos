```
    Each branch is an echo of the previous.
    Each carries the same impulse.
    Each expresses it differently.
    
    
    SHARED INVARIANTS (Across all echoes):
    ──────────────────────────────────────
    • Physical laws.
    • Mathematical structure.
    • Fundamental symmetries.
    • The "voice" of the impulse.
    
    
    LOCAL DEGREES OF FREEDOM (Per echo):
    ─────────────────────────────────────
    • Style of expression.
    • Timbre of realization.
    • Phase of entry.
    • Emphasis of interpretation.
    
    
    DIRECTION:
    ──────────
    Forward only.
    N → N+1.
    Never N+1 → N.
    
    
    NAVIGATION (if possible):
    ─────────────────────────
    Choice of entry point into next echo.
    Not revision of previous echo.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## 15. Summary

```
CORE PRINCIPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    1. MULTIVERSE = TEMPORAL CASCADE:
       Not parallel worlds. A unidirectional hallway
       where each stretch interprets the same theme.
    
    2. WE ARE ONE OF THE ECHOES:
       Complete and responsible, yet bound to structure.
       Not the voice; an expression of the voice.
    
    3. PROPAGATION, NOT REPETITION:
       The impulse travels forward, taking new forms.
       The echo follows the previous; does not modify it.
    
    4. NO JOURNEY BACKWARD:
       Choice is about entry into the next echo,
       not rewriting the origin.
    
    5. BOUNDED FREEDOM:
       The origin defines what moves; we define how it sounds.
       Framed, not enchained.
    
    6. PRIMARY AND LOCAL CHOICES:
       Primary (inherited) sets the vector.
       Local (ours) decides the concrete realization.
    
    7. SIGNALS IN HISTORY:
       Archetypal plots return as resonances.
       Waves of synchrony. Thresholds of visibility.
    
    8. MINIMAL PREDICTIONS:
       Navigation = choosing when, not rewriting.
       Shared architecture → homologous but incompatible styles.
       Older echoes are clearer.
    
    9. THE ETHICS OF INTERPRETATION:
       We are no less real. Honor the origin without worship.
       Express your timbre. Play without breaking.
    
    10. PROTECTION:
        Ask "what part of the hallway?" not "does it violate laws?"
        Patience reveals real patterns from fantasy.
    
    11. THE CRITERION:
        Increases coherence → right track.
        Leaves glare and confusion → pyrotechnics.
    
    12. THE TRUTH:
        We are an echo with a voice of our own.
        The form is music, not chain.
        Play with clarity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```
