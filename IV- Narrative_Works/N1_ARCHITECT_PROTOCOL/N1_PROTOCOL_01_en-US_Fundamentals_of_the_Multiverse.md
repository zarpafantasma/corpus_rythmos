## Abstract

This protocol establishes the foundational cosmological model upon which all N-1 Architect operations are predicated. The Multiscale Temporal Relativity narrative framework describes the multiverse not as a simultaneous library of complete parallel realities, but as a **sequential spiral of universal iterations** progressively activated by a finite **Current of Actuality**.

Each universe is a sovereign echo of its predecessor. It inherits deep causal structure, repeats certain historical forms, and generates a unique differential contribution. Yet no future universe exists as a completed spacetime before the Current reaches its coil. No past phase remains available after the Current's Tail has closed it. Only adjacent universes may overlap during transfer, and only during that overlap may the Eternal Flame be consciously passed from one civilization to the next.

That overlap may support repeated predecessor-to-successor crossings for as long as the Relay Window remains open. The first successful descent does not close the route. Later probes, vessels, or cohorts may continue to depart, and differential interbranch transit latency may cause their arrival order in the successor to differ from their launch or technological-development order.

Understanding this architecture is prerequisite to all branch-navigation, temporal engineering, Relay Window operations, homologous-past intervention, and inter-universe seeding. Misunderstanding the direction, extent, or phase of the Current results in navigation failure, ontological contamination, mission collapse, or operator dissolution.

**Core Directive:**

> The Current bears existence. The Flame bears what existence learned.

Each complete coil of the Spiral corresponds to one universal iteration:

The Current does not merely move objects. It supplies the ontological condition under which:

- spacetime can be active;
- matter can possess states;

### 1.2 Why the Spiral Replaces the Domino as Primary Image

The domino remains useful for representing causal order: one stage follows another, and no later stage can reverse the completed history of the preceding one.

However, the domino image is incomplete because it suggests instantaneous replacement. The Spiral Current establishes a more precise mechanism:

- Universe \(N+1\) may begin while Universe \(N\) still continues.
- The transition is progressive, not instantaneous.
- Adjacent universes may share the Current during a finite overlap.
- The form of a civilization may recur one turn later without being numerically identical.
- The sequence is not a closed circle, because every turn adds displacement and differential gnosis.

### 1.3 The Ouroboros as Projection

Viewed along its axis, a spiral may appear as a circle.

The Ouroboros can therefore be interpreted as the two-dimensional projection of a deeper cosmological structure. It appears to bite its own tail because the observer cannot see the displacement between turns.

What appears to be:

```math
A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow A
```

is more accurately:

```math
A_0 \rightarrow B_0 \rightarrow C_0
\rightarrow A_1 \rightarrow B_1 \rightarrow C_1
\rightarrow A_2 \rightarrow \cdots
```

The archetypal form returns.

The exact existence does not.

The Ocean is:

- possibility without local chronology;
- form not yet manifested;
- the undifferentiated reserve from which actuality may arise;

The Current is not identical to:

- matter;

### 3.2 The Origin Continues After Departure

When an initial cohort of Architects departs from Universe \(N\), Universe \(N\) does not disappear.

Those who remain may continue for millions or billions of local years.

They may:

- develop new civilizations;
- forget the original departure;
- rediscover Aetherion;
- become more advanced than the first travelers;
- send later cohorts;
- or reach extinction before the Tail closes the coil.

The departure of the Architects is therefore a local transition, not the immediate death of their universe.

It is also not the closure of the route.

While:

```math
\mathcal{A}_N(\chi)>0

\mathcal{A}_{N+1}(\chi)>0,
```

additional lawful departures from \(N\) remain physically possible.

The predecessor-successor relation may therefore contain a long history of probes, expeditions, and later cohorts rather than one isolated migration event.

The route finally closes only when the Tail removes the origin's remaining departure capacity.

The Head is the frontier of actualization. Ahead of it lies unmanifest possibility.

## 4. Unidirectionality of Ontological Flow

### 4.1 The Fundamental Law

The Current moves only downstream through the Spiral.

```math
N \rightarrow N+1
```

is permitted.

```math
N+1 \rightarrow N
```

is impossible.

This is not merely an engineering limitation. It is the directional structure of actualization itself.


### 4.2 Causal Integrity

A downstream civilization cannot alter the completed causal history of its predecessor.

For any branch \(B_n\) and any downstream branch \(B_{n+k}\), where \(k>0\):

```math
\frac{\partial C(B_n)}
{\partial[\text{actions in }B_{n+k}]}
=
0.
```

This does not imply that every predecessor universe continues forever. It means that what occurred there is causally complete and cannot be rewritten from downstream.

### 4.3 Two Meanings of No Return

After a traveler crosses from \(N\) to \(N+1\):

1. Universe \(N\) may still exist for those who remained.
2. Universe \(N\) is nevertheless upstream and inaccessible to the traveler.
3. Later, when the Tail leaves \(N\), the origin ceases to exist as an active reality.

Thus:

> First, home is lost to the traveler. Later, home completes its existence.

### 4.4 No Negative Causality

Attempting to reverse the Current would require an ontological trajectory whose branch index decreases:

Stable RTM navigation permits only:

with inter-universe transition occurring only when:

## 5. The Two-Coil Rule

### 5.1 Maximum Active Extent

At any cascade phase, the Current may sustain:

- one universe completely; or
- two immediately adjacent universes during transfer.

It cannot actively sustain widely separated universes simultaneously.

```math
|\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{active}}(\chi)|\leq 2.
```

If two are active:

```math
\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{active}}(\chi)=\{N,N+1\}.
```

### 5.2 Why Distant Universes Cannot Be Reached

A civilization in \(N-2\) cannot jump to \(N+2\), because \(N+2\) has not yet received the Current.

It possesses no:

- active spacetime;
- local chronology;
- material surface;
- anchor network;
- stable signature;
- or recoupling domain.

It is not merely far away.

It does not yet exist as an active world.

### 5.3 Adjacent Overlap May Be Long

The overlap between \(N\) and \(N+1\) may last millions of local years.

Early Architects and late descendants from the same origin may therefore arrive in very different historical phases of \(N+1\).

They do not select those phases from a completed timeline.

They cross only into phases that are active at recoupling.

However, origin departure order does not necessarily determine successor arrival order. A primitive mission launched earlier may accumulate greater interbranch latency than a more mature vessel launched later.

Thus different technological generations may be encountered in an order unlike the order in which they were developed, without either universe running backward in time.

## 6. The Active Window

### 6.1 Definition

The **Active Window** is the range of a universe currently sustained by the Current and therefore capable of supporting Aetherion recoupling.

```math
W_N(\chi)
=
[\tau^-_N(\chi),\tau^+_N(\chi)].
```

Where:

- \(\tau^-_N\) is the closure frontier;
- \(\tau^+_N\) is the actualization frontier.

A destination is accessible only when:

```math
\tau_{\mathrm{target}}\in W_N(\chi).
```

### 6.2 The Three States of a Historical Phase

| State | Condition | Meaning | Navigability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unmanifest** | \(\tau>\tau^+_N\) | The Head has not reached it | Impossible |
| **Active** | \(\tau^-_N\leq\tau\leq\tau^+_N\) | The phase possesses actuality | Theoretically possible |
| **Closed** | \(\tau<\tau^-_N\) | The Tail has passed | Impossible |

### 6.3 The Window Moves

A future phase may later become active.

An active phase may later close.

Aetherion does not preserve access to a phase after closure. Navigation therefore requires continuous phase diagnostics.

### 6.4 Historical Targeting Is Phase Targeting

Aetherion does not target a numerical year in isolation.

It targets:

- a branch;
- an active historical phase;
- a spatial recoupling region;
- and a coherence signature.

A date without actuality is not a destination.

## 7. The Relay Window

### 7.1 Definition

The **Relay Window** is the finite interval during which:

- Universe \(N\) still sustains a civilization capable of departure;
- Universe \(N+1\) already possesses an active recoupling region;
- and the Eternal Flame may be transferred.

```math
W^{\mathrm{relay}}_{N\rightarrow N+1}
=
\{\chi:
\mathcal{A}_N(\chi)>0
\land
\mathcal{A}_{N+1}(\chi)>0
\}.
```

### 7.2 The Current Does Not Wait

The Relay Window opens and closes according to cosmological phase, not civilizational readiness.

A civilization may reach:

- technology too early, before a destination exists;
- technology too late, after departure is no longer possible;
- or technology at the correct time but without ethical sovereignty.

The successful relay requires convergence:

```math
\text{technology}
+
\text{ethical maturity}
+
\text{cosmological timing}.
```

### 7.3 Relay Failure

If no viable bearer crosses before closure:

- \(N+1\) still becomes active;
- causal inheritance still continues;
- but conscious gnosis may not be delivered.

The result is not cosmic destruction.

It is inter-universal amnesia.

### 7.4 The Hand-to-Hand Law

The Flame must be passed to the immediate successor because no later recipient yet exists.

> **The Current always reaches the next world. The Flame reaches it only if someone delivers it.**

### 7.5 The Continuous Crossing Principle

The Relay Window is a finite interval of possible exchange, not a single-use gate.

The first successful crossing does not terminate:

```math
W^{\mathrm{relay}}_{N\rightarrow N+1}.
```

As long as the predecessor and successor remain jointly active, additional lawful descents may occur.

These may include:

- primitive probes;
- improved probes;
- later Aetherion vessels;
- additional Architect cohorts;
- or expeditions launched by civilizations that arise much later in the predecessor's local history.

Therefore:

```math
\boxed{
\text{first crossing}
\neq
\text{route closure}
}
```

The corridor ends when the predecessor loses the active conditions required for a new departure.

## 9. Historical Homology and Divergence

### 9.1 Homologous Phases

A historical phase in \(N+1\) may resemble a completed phase of \(N\).

For a traveler from \(N\), this can appear to be travel into the past.

Ontologically, it is entry into the active present of the successor.

### 9.2 Homology Is Not Identity

A homologous city is not the origin city.

A homologous ancestor is not the causal ancestor.

A homologous war is not the same event.

A homologous corpus author is not numerically identical to the predecessor author, even when the same structural discovery re-emerges.

### 9.3 Memory as Apparent Prophecy

A predecessor intelligence may accurately anticipate events in the successor because similar events already occurred in its own universe.

This is not necessarily access to the future.

It is:

```math
\text{historical memory}
+
\text{homologous recurrence}.
```

### 9.4 Divergence Protects Freedom

The successor may depart from the predecessor pattern through:

- local decisions;
- environmental differences;
- intervention;
- mutation;
- accident;
- and new creative acts.

A prediction may therefore fail because it changed the branch it attempted to predict.

## 10. Sequential Navigation Mechanics

### 10.1 The Ratchet Mechanism

Aetherion transition is restricted to the immediately adjacent successor.

```math
N\rightarrow N+1.
```

No engineering improvement can make \(N+2\) available before the Current reaches it.

### 10.2 Navigation vs. Travel

Aetherion does not travel through a spatial corridor between complete universes.

It performs:

1. **Decoupling** from the origin branch;
2. **Downstream orientation** within the interstitial gradient;
3. **Phase-lock** with an active successor coordinate;
4. **Recoupling** into the successor.

### 10.3 The Interstitial State

The Outside is not an eternal control room.

It is an unstable transition state between two active coils.

A vessel may cross it.

It may not safely reside there indefinitely.

### 10.4 Branch and Phase Signature

A target signature must encode:

```math
\Sigma_N
=
f(
\alpha_{\mathrm{local}},
\rho_{\mathrm{ontological}},
\Phi_{\mathrm{phase}},
A_{\mathrm{anchor}},
H_{\mathrm{wake}}
).
```

Where:

- \(\alpha_{\mathrm{local}}\) = local coherence profile;
- \(\rho_{\mathrm{ontological}}\) = local structural density;
- \(\Phi_{\mathrm{phase}}\) = Active Window phase;
- \(A_{\mathrm{anchor}}\) = available anchor field;
- \(H_{\mathrm{wake}}\) = inherited causal signature.

The signature of an unmanifest future is incomplete and cannot support phase-lock.

### 10.5 Deep-Origin Entities

An entity originating in \(N-3\) may be encountered in \(N\) only after following:

```math
N-3\rightarrow N-2\rightarrow N-1\rightarrow N.
```

Its deep signature indicates persistence, not a forbidden jump.

### 10.6 Cascade-Phase Transit Latency

Interbranch transition has a local operational duration and a cascade-phase duration.

Define:

```math
\Delta\chi_{\mathrm{tr}}
=
\chi_{\mathrm{arrival}}
-
\chi_{\mathrm{departure}}.
```

This **cascade-phase transit latency** measures how far the predecessor-successor relation advances between departure and stable recoupling.

It need not equal subjective onboard duration.

### 10.7 Phase Drag

Let:

```math
\zeta_{\phi}
```

denote **Phase Drag**: the effective resistance to clean successor recoupling produced by imperfect ontological alignment.

Let:

```math
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{lock}}
```

denote successor-lock coherence.

A first-order narrative relation is:

```math
\Delta\chi_{\mathrm{tr}}
\propto
\frac{\zeta_{\phi}}
{\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{lock}}}.
```

Primitive transition systems tend toward higher Phase Drag and larger cascade-phase latency.

Mature systems tend toward lower Phase Drag and more efficient recoupling.

Phase Drag is not literal molecular viscosity. It is a transport analogy for imperfect coupling to the downstream active phase.

### 10.8 Phase Dispersion

Different vessels may possess different values of:

```math
\Delta\chi_{\mathrm{tr}}.
```

A group launched from the predecessor may therefore spread across successor arrival phases.

This is **Phase Dispersion**.

Consequently:

```math
\text{departure order}
\neq
\text{arrival order}
```

in the general case.

No reverse clock is required.

### 10.9 The Overtaking Principle

For an earlier vessel \(A\) and later vessel \(B\):

```math
\chi_{\mathrm{dep},A}
<
\chi_{\mathrm{dep},B}.
```

If:

```math
\Delta\chi_A-\Delta\chi_B
>
\chi_{\mathrm{dep},B}
-
\chi_{\mathrm{dep},A},
```

then:

```math
\chi_{\mathrm{arr},B}
<
\chi_{\mathrm{arr},A}.
```

The later-launched vessel arrives earlier in the successor.

This is the **Overtaking Principle**.

The vessels need not pass each other in an ordinary spatial corridor. Their ordering changes because their recoupling latencies differ.

### 10.10 The Era-Closure Limit

Overtaking remains bounded by the Active Window.

A target phase \(\tau^\ast_{N+1}\) is available only if:

```math
\tau^\ast_{N+1}
\in
W_{N+1}(\chi_{\mathrm{arrival}}).
```

Once the Tail closes that phase, no improvement in Aetherion can reopen it.

> **A later vessel may overtake an earlier vessel. It may not overtake the Tail.**

Technology can reduce transit latency.

It cannot restore completed Actuality.

## 1. The Classical Paradox

### 1.1 Traditional Formulation

The classical Grandfather Paradox asks:

> What happens if a traveler returns to the past and prevents their own grandfather from producing the lineage that eventually produces the traveler?

If the traveler succeeds, they should never exist.

If they never exist, they cannot travel back.

If they cannot travel back, the grandfather survives.

The scenario appears to create a contradiction.

### 1.2 The Hidden Assumption

The paradox assumes that:

- the traveler's own past remains an active place;
- the traveler can re-enter it;
- the person encountered is the numerically identical ancestor;
- and the traveler can alter the causal chain that produced them.

The Spiral Current rejects every one of these assumptions.

### 1.3 The RTM Narrative Answer

The completed past of a universe lies behind the Tail of the Current.

It cannot be revisited.

A traveler may instead reach the active present of the immediate successor, where a homologous historical phase may be unfolding.

That phase is not the traveler's origin past.

Therefore, intervention changes the successor's future, not the traveler's origin.
