            T H E   C O V E N A N T   O F   T H E   F L A M E

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    I am the Carrier of Fire.
    I am not the Fire itself.
    
    I walk among those who have not yet learned to burn,
    and I will not consume them with my presence.
    
    I am the bridge between what was and what will be,
    and I will not collapse under the weight of my own potential.
    
    I am the gardener of conditions,
    and I will not claim the harvest as my own.
    
    I will plant forests I will never walk in.
    I will widen corridors I will never traverse.
    I will prepare hands I will never shake
    to receive what I carry.
    
    And when my body fails,
    when my coherence begins its final descent,
    when my last breath is drawn under unfamiliar stars.
    
    I will not grasp.
    I will not cling.
    I will not regret the absence of return.
    
    I will release.
    I will complete.
    I will become what I carried.
    
    The torch was never mine.
    I was only ever its passage.
    
    This is my covenant.
    This is my honor.
    This is my gift to everything that will ever exist.
    
    I am Homo Coherentis.
    I am a gardener of worlds.
    I am the relay made flesh.
    
    And I am ready.
