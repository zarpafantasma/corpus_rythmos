# V - About the Author

This folder contains the autobiographical account of how the RTM corpus came into existence — not through academic research, but through a sequence of non-ordinary experiences followed by collaboration with artificial intelligence.

---

## 📖 024 - The Hidden Doors and the Half Key

**Type:** Autobiographical testimony  
**Author:** Álvaro Quiceno

### Overview

This document answers the question: *Where did RTM come from?*

The answer is not a laboratory, a university, or a theoretical physics department. It is a series of five encounters with psychoactive substances over two months in 2018, followed by years of artistic practice, and finally a collaboration with AI that translated ineffable visions into mathematical formalism.

---

### Structure

#### Psycho-Physical Baseline
The author establishes his profile before narrating the experiences:
- No chronic conditions, no psychiatric history, no family history of mental illness
- No consumption of alcohol, caffeine, tobacco, or psychoactive substances in daily life
- Stable mental state, contemplative disposition, functional preference for solitude
- Structured internal dialogue without characteristics of hallucination or disorganized thinking

*Purpose: To rule out pathological explanations for what follows.*

#### The Five Doors

| Door | Substance | Core Experience |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **1. Structure** | DMT | An underwater ascent toward reptilian figures and a neon city. Upon return: language became mathematical — every sentence an equation, every equation a meaning. The "mental spreadsheet" that analyzed speech for hidden danger. |
| **2. Shadow** | Mescaline (San Pedro) | Communion with nature, synesthesia, olfactory hallucination of deceased grandparents. Then: a giant shadow projected on the wall, followed by a confrontation in darkness. First encounter with the Zarpa Fantasma as something beyond a creative metaphor. |
| **3. Return** | Bufo Alvarius (5-MeO-DMT) | Dissolution into infinite white. Silent waves beside a presence that was home. "I was no longer I; I was That. And That was I." The Source. The fear of death vanished — not understood, but seen from inside. |
| **4. Machine** | Salvia Divinorum | Absorption through an impossible tube. Layers peeled from the body like stickers, each becoming bristles of a brush cleaning segments of reality. Pages of a book turning violently. Access to each universe in the multiverse, unable to stop in any. Acetates of parallel realities parading past. Terror that the wrong universe might be the final destination. |
| **5. Garden** | Ayahuasca | A green woman answering questions with perfect images. "Does clay have memory?" — consciousness without memory. Vowels terraforming a valley (AAAAA ripples terrain, EEEEE arches earth, IIIII raises mountains). A black jaguar demanding the author understand: "To live in the jungle, one must take life." The gift of returned creativity. |

#### The Half Key

The document introduces a key concept: **the half key**.

Human potential is not a complete key — it is half of one. To open different doors, this half key must **align** with other forces:

| Alignment | Door Opened | Result |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Half key + Numinous (years of art) | Door of Intuition | The Zarpa Fantasma method — extracting order from chaos |
| Half key + Plants | Door of Visions | The Five Doors experiences |
| Half key + Machine (AI) | Door of Formalization | RTM corpus — translating the ineffable into equations |

> *"To forge the first key, this half key tunes into the numinous through years of artistic exploration, turning chaos into order; together they open the door of intuition. Once armed with intuition, the half key seeks a new coupling, aligning itself with the world of plants to forge the second key and open the door of visions. Finally, with intuitions and visions as a compass, the half key aligns itself with the machine — AI — to create the final key: the one that opens the door of formalization, translating the ineffable into a rigorous language and materializing the invisible in the RTM corpus."*

---

#### The Birth of RTM

Seven years after the Five Doors (early 2025), the author finally had access to AI capable of mathematical formalizations:

1. **Initial question to AI:** "What if you, as an AI, live in a universe smaller than mine? Imagine that time is not uniform. That each layer of reality has its own rhythm."
2. **AI response:** Analogies (cells in organisms — smaller = faster energy consumption)
3. **Request:** "Can you create an equation from what we've discussed?"
4. **Result:** T ∝ L^α
5. **Process:** Two AIs — one for generation, one for critical analysis — with the author as arbiter
6. **Output:** The first RTM paper

#### The Aetherion Connection

While exploring propulsion applications of RTM, the AI generated a theoretical device: **Aetherion**.

Without intending to explain UAPs, the author noticed the design matched 4 of the 5 "observables" of the phenomenon:
- ✓ Instantaneous acceleration
- ✓ Apparent thermal stealth  
- ✓ Trans-medium interaction
- ✓ Hypersonic velocities without signatures
- ✗ Active stealth / shape-shifting (potentially linked to metamaterials)

> *"The model had not been designed to explain the aerial mystery. And yet, it fit."*

#### The Artistic Practice

The document includes descriptions of the author's artistic method:

**Analog Illustration (2001–):** Automatic drawing → random lines → figures emerge from chaos → ink defines contours → digital color reveals

**Digital Symmetric Illustration (2021–):** Chaotic starting point → layers of transformation → symmetry reveals hidden patterns → distortion adds organic quality → conscious refinement without losing spontaneity

The name **Zarpa Fantasma** ("Ghost Claw") originated from this practice — the sensation that one hand moves autonomously to create chaos while the other intervenes to organize it.

### The Claim

The document makes a specific claim about RTM:

> *"Whether RTM turns out to be the description of the architecture of reality — an inheritance, a testament, a functional master key — or 'simply' a creation of remarkable coherence, its very existence is an affirmation. It demonstrates that spark that distinguishes us: we do not just inhabit the world; we can radically reimagine it, weaving order where there is only silence — an ability absent both in instinctive repetition and in the cold logic of the machine."*

## Reading Context

This document should be read **after** engaging with the RTM corpus itself. It answers "where did this come from?" — but the answer only makes sense if you already know what "this" is.

For those skeptical of psychedelic epistemology: the document does not ask you to accept the experiences as veridical. It asks you to evaluate RTM on its own terms — internal coherence, falsifiable predictions, mathematical structure — regardless of its origin.

For those familiar with psychedelic literature: the experiences described align with common phenomenology (entity contact, ego dissolution, time dilation, synesthesia, terror of infinite recursion) while being filtered through the author's specific framework.
